# Third-party data notice

## Egypt outline

`egypt.geo.json` is the Egypt feature filtered from Natural Earth’s 1:50m Admin-0 Countries dataset, using the v4.0.0-era GeoJSON conversion at the pinned `martynafford/natural-earth-geojson` commit. The geometry is retained in geographic longitude/latitude coordinates; only unrelated country features and unused attributes were removed.

Natural Earth map data is public domain. The GeoJSON mirror publishes its data under CC0 1.0 Universal. The official Natural Earth citation, when desired, is: “Made with Natural Earth.”
